A two-color risograph print illustration, only two inks: medium blue (#3255a4) and fluorescent pink (#ff48b0), printed on warm cream uncoated paper (#f4efe4); where the two inks overlap they make a deep purple. Flat shapes, coarse halftone dots for shading, slight ink grain and imperfect stencil edges, like a screen-printed gig poster. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos anywhere. Clean paper margin around the print edge.

Poster scene for a 1949 Shanghai film about tenants of one house: cross-section of a crowded three-storey shikumen house seen from the front, many small rooms with lit windows, laundry lines, a staircase, tiny silhouettes of tenants without faces. On the roof ridge a big black crow perches, and small sparrows sit on the window sills and wires. Vertical poster composition, busy but readable.
