A two-color risograph print illustration, only two inks: medium blue (#3255a4) and fluorescent pink (#ff48b0), printed on warm cream uncoated paper (#f4efe4); where the two inks overlap they make a deep purple. Flat shapes, coarse halftone dots for shading, slight ink grain and imperfect stencil edges, like a screen-printed gig poster. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos anywhere. Clean paper margin around the print edge.

Poster scene for a 1937 Shanghai film: two old shikumen houses facing each other across a very narrow alley at night, seen from high up between the rooftops. Two open windows face each other, warm pink light inside, a small orange-like fruit flying across the gap from one window to the other in an arc, laundry poles and tiled roofs all around. Figures in the windows are tiny silhouettes with no faces. Vertical poster composition.
