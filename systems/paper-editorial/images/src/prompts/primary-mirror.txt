A copperplate etching illustration, as if printed in a 19th-century scientific atlas. Inside a clean room of a mountaintop observatory, three technicians in hooded cleanroom suits stand around a 1.6-metre telescope primary mirror resting upright in a steel handling cradle. One leans close and inspects the mirror surface with a small torch, one steadies the cradle, one kneels to check a mounting bolt. The mirror is a thick glass disc; its polished face reflects the overhead lamps and the technicians' silhouettes as soft curved highlights. Scale matters: the mirror is taller than the kneeling person.
