A small classical oil painting vignette: a weathered Minnan fisherman in his seventies mending a net on a wooden crate at a harbour, mid-sentence, a cigarette tucked behind his ear. A small voice recorder sits on the crate next to the net; the recorder is the only modern object. Loose painterly background of boats and sea haze, generous paper margin all around.
Style: a genuine classical oil painting on linen canvas, visible confident brushwork, soft north-window light from the upper left, muted earthy palette (umber, ochre, olive #8e8a5a, sage #cddfd6, deep teal #2e6b69 accents), believable dignified postures, faces painted with care. The modern device is NOT painted in oil: it is rendered only as crisp black-and-white halftone dots, like a photo printed in a 1950s newspaper, clearly a different material from the paint, with no colour at all. At its edges a few halftone dots scatter outward and turn into tiny deep-teal (#2e6b69) square pixels that fade out. The painting sits on warm off-white paper (#f7f3ea) with a fine grain and fades softly into the paper at the margins instead of having a hard frame. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
