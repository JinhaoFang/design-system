Dusk in the mountains: layered cut-paper ridges in cobalt, indigo and pine teal, a narrow persimmon-coloured road winding up as a ribbon to a tiny white dome on the summit, a large mustard sun disc setting behind the peaks, a few peach clouds.
Style: a collage of hand-cut paper in the spirit of mid-century gouache cut-outs: sheets of paper brushed with flat gouache (a faint visible brush texture inside each colour), cut freehand with scissors into simplified, slightly irregular organic shapes with blunt rounded corners, pasted flat; no outlines, no gradients, no 3D rendering, only an extremely faint shadow showing paper thickness. Palette strictly limited to: vermilion #d9482b, persimmon #ee8a4a, peach #f3b6aa, mustard #e3ac2e, cobalt #2c56ad, indigo #1b2c63, pine teal #1f8a7a, sprout green #95bf62, plus the background. Background is plain warm light grey paper (#ecebe6). People, if any, are simplified flat cut-out silhouettes with no facial features. Calm, airy composition with lots of empty grey paper, poster-like clarity. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
